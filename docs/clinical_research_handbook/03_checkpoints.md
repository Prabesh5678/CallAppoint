# 3. The 5 Checkpoints & Their Necessity

## Checkpoint 1: The Patient Baseline (Lifestyle)
- **What:** Smoking, Alcohol, Family History.
- **Why:** Researchers need to identify "Risk Factors." You can't study lung cancer without knowing if the patient smokes.

## Checkpoint 2: The Diagnosis Engine (ICD & TNM)
- **What:** Universal codes (ICD-10) and Cancer Staging (TNM).
- **Why:** Every hospital in the world uses these. It makes our data "Interoperable."

## Checkpoint 3: The Biomarker Tracker
- **What:** Numeric values (e.g., PSA: 4.5 ng/mL).
- **Why:** Charts! We can now draw a line graph showing a patient getting better over time.

## Checkpoint 4: The Precision Layer (Genomics)
- **What:** Gene mutations (e.g., BRCA1).
- **Why:** This is the future of medicine. We can study "Targeted Therapies."

## Checkpoint 5: Analytics Dashboards
- **What:** Aggregated charts for the whole hospital.
- **Why:** It gives the hospital "Big Picture" insights immediately.
