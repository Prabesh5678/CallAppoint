# 4. User Guide: How to use the System

## For Doctors (The Scientist)
Doctors no longer just "write notes." They "record data."
1. **During Appointment:** The doctor opens the patient's Clinical Profile.
2. **Diagnosis:** They pick an ICD-10 code and select the T, N, and M stages from dropdowns.
3. **Lab Results:** Instead of just looking at the PDF, they type in the numeric value (e.g., "Creatinine: 1.2").
4. **Genetics:** They record any mutations found in the lab.

## For Patients (The Participant)
1. **My Journey:** Patients see a "Health Trend" graph instead of just a list of files.
2. **Profile:** They fill in their lifestyle habits once, so every doctor they see already knows their history.
3. **Safety:** They can see their genetic results, but they cannot edit them—ensuring the record remains official.

## For Researchers (The Analyst)
1. **Analytics:** They hit the `/analytics/summary/` endpoint.
2. **Output:** They get a breakdown of all stages and genetic variants across the entire patient population instantly.
