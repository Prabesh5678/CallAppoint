# 1. The Problem: Unstructured Clinical Data

## The "Monkey" Explanation
Imagine a library where every book is just a pile of random loose papers thrown into a box. If you want to find out how many books mention "Apples," you have to read every single page in every single box. That's how traditional medical apps work—they just store big text notes or PDF files.

## What we faced
In the original **CallAppoint** system:
- **Medical Reports** were just PDF links.
- **Diagnoses** were just a single text box where doctors could write anything they wanted.
- **Biomarkers** (like blood sugar or tumor levels) were hidden inside PDFs.

## The Research Gap
Researchers couldn't answer simple questions like:
- *"How many Stage III cancer patients are smokers?"*
- *"Does this specific genetic mutation respond better to the treatment?"*

Computers can't "read" PDFs and random notes easily. We needed **Structured Data**.
